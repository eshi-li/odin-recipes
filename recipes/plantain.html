plantain recipe

ingredients

  • plantain
  • 1 tbale spoon of salt
  • 2 cups of oil
  1. peel you plantain and slice it into any size of your desire sprinkle it with 1 tb of salt
  2. put the 2 cups of oil in the pan and heat it up for 5 minutes then put your sliced platains into the pan
  3. after 10 minutes remove them and use any sauce of your desires
